# Colorado benefit programs: where the official answer lives

This skill tells you **where to look**. It never gives the answer. Always open the official page and read the current number there.

## General rules
- Colorado state sites end in `colorado.gov` or `state.co.us`. The official Health First Colorado member site is `healthfirstcolorado.com`.
- Colorado's own regulations are in the Code of Colorado Regulations, published by the Colorado Secretary of State (`sos.state.co.us`).
- Federal regulations are on `ecfr.gov`. Federal laws are in the U.S. Code.
- The federal poverty guidelines are published by HHS (`aspe.hhs.gov`). Many limits are a percent of these guidelines, and they are updated once a year.
- Colorado's online application portal is PEAK (on `colorado.gov`).
- Federal programs run by the state: Colorado may use options federal law allows, so check the Colorado page before using a federal default table.

## Tax credits
- Tax credit questions are always about a specific tax year. Use the IRS or Colorado Department of Revenue page for that exact tax year.
- Colorado tax credits are set by state law and can change or end by year. Check whether the credit exists for the tax year asked.
